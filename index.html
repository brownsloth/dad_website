His story

A quiet influence, a lasting reach.

Brij Kishore Sharma spent the better part of his professional life teaching high school physics in Jaipur — not simply as equations to be memorised, but as a way of observing the world with patience and reason.

His students remember a teacher who never rushed an explanation, who believed that every confused student was one clear example away from understanding, and whose composure in difficult moments felt more instructive than any lesson plan.

In his later years he moved into school leadership as a principal, then took on a broader role as a district literacy officer — working to strengthen the relationship between schools, families, and the wider community they serve.

Now retired from formal service, he is exploring a new direction: sharing what a lifetime in education can offer to conversations about science, public literacy, media, and thoughtful citizenship. He is polished, unhurried, and genuinely worth listening to.
